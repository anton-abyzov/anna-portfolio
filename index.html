About Me

Hi! I'm Anna, and I'm passionate about numbers, music, and making learning an inspiring experience.

As Math Olympic team captain, I led our team to regional victory. Mathematics is like solving puzzles that unlock the universe.

I'm an international piano laureate who loves bringing classical masterpieces to life. From Chopin to Rachmaninoff, every piece tells a story.

Teaching is my calling. I help students discover that learning can be exciting and deeply rewarding.
